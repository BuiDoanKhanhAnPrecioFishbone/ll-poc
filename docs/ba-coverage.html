Shape and elevation — the radius scale is shifted a whole step

Hers against ours, side by side:

Every value she names exists here; every name points one step lower. The consequence is concrete: she puts "inputs, value boxes, buttons, icon buttons, banners, toasts" at 6px, and this system draws them at 4px. Nothing is broken, everything is slightly tighter than she drew.

Also from this section:

  • The 3px blur behind an open popup is not implemented. There is no backdrop-filter anywhere in this system. Her overlay is rgba(19,24,32,.32) + blur 3px; ours is rgba(19,24,32,.45) and no blur — darker, to do the same job the blur does for her.
  • Her z-index list matches ours exactly where both name a layer: drawer 10020, popup 10040, menu 10070, toast 12500, skip 12600. Her two lowest, z-sticky 2 and z-toolbar 10, have no token here.
  • She has three shadows; this system has five. Different systems rather than a disagreement.

Large editable grid — a second interaction mode, entirely unbuilt

For grids over ~200 lines she specifies that editable cells draw no box at all until hover, keeping only a changed cell boxed in blue until Save. Small grids inside a form keep a visible box in every editable cell.

This system has one mode. Note this is also the section that produces one of the open accessibility questions — "editable and read-only cells look the same until hover" cannot work on the shop-floor terminals and tablets her own Screen sizes section names.
