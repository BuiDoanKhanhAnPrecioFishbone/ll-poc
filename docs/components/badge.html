Status badge

src/ui/Badge.tsx · specimen at /design-system

When to use

To show what state a record is in — on a record header, in a status column, in an activity row. One word and one colour, together.

When not to use

Instead of a status badgeUseWhy
A count, a tag or a label that is not a lifecycle state.vy-pill17 hand-written call sites already do this. A badge carries the status vocabulary; a pill carries anything else
A mode the screen is in, rather than a state the record is indata-token="editing" by handSee The eighth token below
Something the user can click or changeA chip, a select, a buttonA badge is output. Nothing about it is operable
PriorityPriorityA dot and a word, on its own scale (B5.3 gives it 104px)

Anatomy

A single <span>. The word is the content — there is no icon, no dot, and nothing hidden from a screen reader, because a status that is only a colour is not a status (E1).

PartRule
Container--vy-status-slot-w = 104px fixed, --vy-shape-full. The fixed width is the point: a status column scans as a straight edge instead of a ragged one. This was GAP-01 against the old library; with our own component it is one token
Label--vy-text-xs at --vy-weight-semibold, never wraps

The vocabulary

Seven tokens, and what they encode is who is blocked — not what stage the record has reached.

TokenMeansLive words that map to it
draftnothing has happened yetDraft, Pending
openwaiting on us — this is what a work queue filters toNew, Open
progressactively being worked by usIn-Progress, Partial
waitingwaiting on someone else — a customer, a supplier, an approverQuoted, Submitted, Awaiting Approval
doneclosed successfullyActive, Released, Completed, Paid
blockedstopped, needs interventionObsolete, Blocked, Overdue
cancelledclosed without completingInactive, Cancelled, Closed
Seven, not six. waiting was added after the Quotations screen showed why six is one short: an RFQ that is New (nobody has picked it up) and one that is Quoted (sent, awaiting the customer) are opposite states — one needs you today, one does not — and both collapsed onto open and rendered the same amber. Two distinct states sharing a colour is the defect the production Part Master has, where every status is green.

An unknown word falls back to draft. STATUS_TOKEN[value] ?? 'draft' — so a lifecycle word nobody has mapped renders grey rather than throwing. That is the right failure, and it is also silent: a new status word will look deliberately neutral instead of looking wrong.

The eighth token, and a ninth that cannot be reached

  • editing is a mode, not a status. Blue, because blue means changed, which is what the record is while it shows. It is written by hand — QuotationDetail.tsx:476 — and is deliberately not in STATUS_TOKEN, so StatusBadge can never produce it.
  • review has colours and no way to render them. --vy-status-review-fg and -bg exist in tokens.css and are documented in B1.6, but there is no STATUS_TOKEN entry and no .vy-badge[data-token="review"] rule. The specimen page explains why — cyan belongs to a checklist document awaiting approval, reached through the task state on the Checklists tab, not through this badge — but a reader of tokens.css alone cannot tell that the pair is intentionally unreachable from here.

Contrast

Every pair is measured on every push by contrast:check. Light theme, each foreground on its own background:

cancelled is the tightest in the set and still clears 4.5. In dark every pair sits between 6.16 and 9.30.

progress only got a dark pair on 8 Nov 2026. Before that it kept its light colours and showed as one bright cyan chip on a dark grid.

Behaviour

Nothing. The badge has no states: no hover, no focus, no disabled. It is not focusable and not operable. If a status needs to be changed, the control that changes it is a separate thing beside it.

Accessibility

NameThe visible word is the accessible name. No aria-label, no title
ColourNever carries meaning alone — the word is always present. This is the rule E1 lists first
RoleNone. A <span> of text is what a status is
ContrastMeasured per pair, above

API

<StatusBadge value={q.status} />
PropTypeDefaultNotes
valuestring—A live lifecycle word. Unknown values render as draft

The status column role renders this automatically — renderCell.tsx maps data-role="status" to it, so a grid never writes the badge by hand.

One thing worth fixing

.vy-pill has 17 hand-written call sites and no component. Pill lived in Badge.tsx and nothing ever rendered it, so it was removed with a note rather than silently. The callers to convert, if it comes back, are in PartDetail, ChecklistsTab, ConversationsTab and StepSummary. Until then a pill is a class, which means nothing stops two screens drawing it differently.
