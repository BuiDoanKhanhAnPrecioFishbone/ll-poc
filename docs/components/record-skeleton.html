Record skeleton

src/ui/RecordSkeleton.tsx · the first-load state of a record form, as GridSkeleton is for the list.

When to use

While one record's values are loading, on a screen whose fields are already known.

When not to use

Instead of thisUseWhy
A save in progressBusyOverlayThe values are on screen; they are being sent, not fetched
A new, empty recordThe form itselfThere is nothing to wait for

The labels are real, and only the values are bars

A skeleton greys out what it does not know yet — and on a record screen that is less than people assume.

The labels, the grouping and the order are in the field declarations, which are already in the bundle. It is the customer's name and the due date that are still in flight. Greying the labels as well would be hiding something we are holding, and it would cost the reader the one thing that makes a wait bearable: knowing what they are about to see.

So Customer, Project and Schedule & ownership read normally from the first frame.

Nothing moves when the data arrives — the labels are already in their final positions, which is the whole promise a skeleton makes and the usual one it breaks.

It is built from the same declarations the form is

It takes HEADER_GROUPS, the same array QuotationDetail renders from.

A field added to the form appears here too, and the skeleton cannot drift into describing a screen that no longer exists.

Bars are sized by field kind (data-sk), the same idea as the grid's widths by column role — a date's placeholder is a date's width, and a notes field gets two bars.

It wears the record's own container classes

vy-rfq-head, vy-action-bar, vy-record-bar, vy-back, vy-record-title — rather than a parallel set with the numbers copied across.

Borrowing the class is exact; copying the number is exact until someone edits the original.

This was found by measuring, not by looking:

AttemptWhat happened
FirstThe top rows were skipped entirely, so the field groups sat 92px too high — when the record arrived the whole form jumped down, breaking the one promise a skeleton makes, in the same file whose comment claims it keeps it
SecondStill 24px out. 18 of those were margins living on vy-back and vy-record-title, so those two classes are worn as well
