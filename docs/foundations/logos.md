# Logos

**There is no new mark yet.** This page says what exists, what was decided,
what is open and what is on the shortlist. It has no clear-space, minimum-size
or misuse rules, because those belong to a mark someone has drawn.

The source is `docs/logo-ideas.md`, started 26 September 2026, which opens:
"Nothing here is built yet; this is the shortlist to choose from."

## What exists

The current mark is the blue tile with the letter `V`. Decision **D3**
(7 September) said a new mark comes later and to keep the `V` in the meantime.

Measured in the current build, the mark appears at:

| Place | Size |
|---|---|
| Sidebar, beside the name; collapsed, alone | 28×28 |
| Login screen, on the dark panel | 28×28 |
| Phone drawer | 28×28 |
| Browser tab (favicon) | 16×16 |

Small, square, on light and dark. Printed quotes, packing lists and email are
possible later uses; their size is unknown and is a question for the client.

## What is fixed

- The name is `VOYAGER IQ`, with `Linh Long Engineering` under it.
- The blue is set: hue 222, primary `#1a419e`, derived from the existing mark (D1).
- Red is reserved for destructive actions, so the mark is not red.
- The typeface is Inter (D5).
- Rocket EMS colours are out (D2). The loading illustration is a rocket by a
  deliberate exception confirmed 27 Oct 2026; it uses Voyager IQ's own blues.

## The five tests

Readable at 16px. Works in one colour. Works on dark. Not confusable with the
sidebar's 12 line icons. Says something true: navigation and accuracy.

## The shortlist

| | Direction | Risk named in the source |
|---|---|---|
| A | The `V` tile, drawn properly | It is what they have |
| B | The waypoint: a `V` that is also a needle or pin | Pins are common |
| C | The `V` with a dot at the vertex | Dots read as notifications |
| D | The route: three nodes forming a `V`, quote → build → ship | May smudge at 16px |
| E | Container and monogram, the `V` cut out of a rounded square | Generic |
| F | The chevron stack, reading as BoM levels | Looks like a collapse arrow |

## Still open

Three questions for the client: where else the mark appears, whether it sits
beside the Rocket EMS logo, and whether a brand file exists. The recorded next
step is to draw A, B and D at 16, 28 and 512px, in light and dark, on the real
sidebar and login screen, and then choose. None of that is done.

Ruled out: gears and circuit traces, globes and swooshes, a rocket, and text
inside the mark. Full reasoning: [logo ideas](../logo-ideas.md).
