# The Voyager IQ mark — research and first ideas

Started 26 September 2026. Nothing here is built yet; this is the shortlist to
choose from before anything is drawn properly.

Decision D3 (7 September) said a new mark comes **later**, and to keep the
letter `V` in the meantime. Later has arrived, so this is the groundwork.

---

## 1. Where the mark actually appears

Measured in the current build, because the smallest place it appears is the
one that decides the design.

| Place | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sidebar, beside the name | 28×28 | The one users see all day |
| Sidebar, collapsed | 28×28 | No name beside it — the mark is alone |
| Login screen | 28×28 on the dark panel | Must work on dark |
| Browser tab (favicon) | 16×16 | Smallest, and unforgiving |
| Phone drawer | 28×28 | Same mark, touch layout |
| Later: printed quotes, packing lists, email | unknown | Question for the client |

**So the mark is small and square, and it lives on both light and dark.** That
rules out anything with fine lines or text inside it.

## 2. What is already fixed

- **The name is `VOYAGER IQ`**, with `Linh Long Engineering` under it.
- **The blue is set** — hue 222, primary `#1a419e`, derived from the existing
  mark so the brand equity carries over (decision D1).
- **The red is the accent** from the live logo, and it is reserved for
  destructive actions. A red mark would spend that meaning.
- **Typeface is Inter** (D5). A wordmark drawn from Inter needs no licence.
- **Rocket EMS colours are out** (D2) — Voyager IQ is its own identity.

## 3. The five tests every idea has to pass

1. **Readable at 16px.** If it needs detail to work, it does not work.
2. **Works in one colour.** For a fax, a stamp, an engraving, a dark theme.
3. **Works on dark.** The login panel and the sidebar are dark.
4. **Not confusable with our own icons.** The sidebar already carries 12 line
   icons; the mark must not read as a thirteenth.
5. **Says something true.** Navigation and accuracy, not "technology".

## 4. Six directions

### A. The `V` tile, refined *(lowest risk)*
Keep the blue tile and the `V`, but draw the letter properly instead of setting
it in Inter — even weight, flat cut, optical centring.
*Good:* nothing is lost, and it ships in a day. *Risk:* it is what they have.

### B. The waypoint
A `V` that is also a map pin or a compass needle pointing up. "Voyager" is a
navigation word; the product's job is telling an estimator where a job is.
*Good:* the name and the mark finally mean the same thing. *Risk:* pins are
common — it has to be geometric, not a location pin.

### C. The `V` with a dot
The `V` with a single dot at the vertex, or above it. The dot is the decision
point — the `IQ` half of the name.
*Good:* a very small change that becomes a system: the dot can appear alone as
a favicon, a status, a loading mark. *Risk:* dots read as notifications.

### D. The route
Three nodes joined into a path that forms a `V`: quote → build → ship. This is
the sentence already on our login screen — *"One record for a part, from quote
to shipment."*
*Good:* it says what the product does, not what industry it is in.
*Risk:* three nodes at 16px may become a smudge; needs testing early.

### E. Container and monogram
A rounded square "window" with the `V` cut out of it rather than sitting on it.
This is the safe, current pattern for B2B software marks — it gives an app
icon for free.
*Good:* consistent everywhere, no separate icon needed. *Risk:* generic; the
`V` has to carry all the character.

### F. The chevron stack
Two or three chevrons stacked into a `V`, reading as BoM levels — an assembly
made of parts made of parts.
*Good:* the one idea that is specific to electronics manufacturing.
*Risk:* looks like a "collapse" arrow in a UI full of them.

## 5. What we will not do

- **Gears, circuit traces, PCB patterns.** Every manufacturing-software logo
  has them; they say "factory" and nothing else.
- **Globes, swooshes, orbit rings.** These were the 2010 ERP look.
- **A rocket.** Rocket EMS is the parent company — a rocket would read as their
  mark, and D2 says Voyager IQ stands on its own.
- **Text inside the mark.** At 16px, letters other than the initial disappear.

## 6. Three questions for the client

1. **Where else does the mark appear?** Quote PDFs, packing lists, email
   signatures, factory signage? A mark that has to be printed in one colour is
   a different brief from one that only lives on screen.
2. **Does it ever sit beside the Rocket EMS logo?** If yes, the two need to
   agree in weight and height.
3. **Is there an existing brand file?** A vector of the current logo, or a
   typeface the company already licenses, saves guessing.

## 7. Next step

Draw **A, B and D** properly — the safe one, the meaningful one and the
ambitious one — at 16px, 28px and 512px, in light and dark, and place them on
the real sidebar and login screen rather than on a presentation board. Then
choose.
