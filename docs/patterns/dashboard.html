Two screens, and the reason they stayed apart

AsksLives at
HomeHow much work came in, and when/ — sequence 1 in the live menu
My QueuesIs there something here for me/my-queues, its own nav entry

An earlier version of this build replaced Home with the work queue. Decision D6 reversed it.

Home is the one screen every user has already learned. Spending it to add a screen they have not is a trade that looks free and is not.

So the queues got their own entry, and Home does what the live Home does. What was removed from it is the stock Kendo demo chart the production screen still carries — World Population by Broad Age Groups, sample data, shipped.

Home — one chart, and it says what it does not know

Twelve stacked bars, five series, two filters. Drawn in CSS: a chart dependency buys axes and tooltips this screen does not use, and costs a second colour system that no longer answers to the tokens.

RuleWhy
A month with no requests keeps its columnA gap in a time series is information. Dropping empty months makes an eight-month year look like a busy one
The total is a sentence, not a caption220 requests in 2026 — and when a filter empties it, Nothing was raised in 2026 for …, naming the filter rather than drawing an empty axis
Every count is printedUnder its own bar, and again in the legend. A bar you have to measure against a gridline is a bar you will misread
Zero is dimmed, not hiddendata-zero drops it to the subtle tier, so the twelve months still read as twelve
The series order is the lifecycleNew → In-Progress → Quoted → Completed → Cancelled, so a stack reads bottom-to-top as work progressing

The one inline style this repo allows is here. Every bar's height is a share of the peak month, which no stylesheet can know, so the component sets a custom property — --seg-h — and the rule that uses it stays in app.css. Inline styling is banned because it hides design decisions from the token layer; a data value handed to a rule that lives in the stylesheet does not.

And the page says the chart is inferred. The live chart renders from an API response, so its exact series and filter set are not in the shipped bundle the way the Run Quotation strings are. The shape here is read off the RFQ record. It is the one thing in this prototype resting on inference rather than evidence, and it carries a note saying so — A3 rule 5.

Colour in a chart is not colour in a badge

This is the part that went wrong twice, and the second time it went wrong because of the first fix.

The status tokens come in pairs: -fg is ink, -bg is the pale chip behind it. A chart segment is neither — it is a shape with no text on it, and it needs to be visible against the card.

What happened
FirstThe chart read -fg for all five. --vy-status-progress-fg was #ffffff at the time, so In-Progress was drawn white on a white card — 39 records, invisible, and the legend key beside it blank
The fixThat one series switched to --vy-status-progress-bg
Then the tokens movedThe pairs were restructured to the convention above. progress-fg became #155e75 — the saturated half, like its four siblings — and the exception was never revisited
Measured, 9 Nov#cffafe on the light card is 1.12:1. In dark it is #1e3838 on #161c25: 1.37:1

Same series, same invisibility, opposite cause. It survived nine weeks because nothing measured a fill that carries no text — every pair in contrast:check was ink on a ground or an edge on a ground.

A fix that outlives the bug it fixed is worse than no fix: it reads as a decision, so nobody re-opens it.

Every series now reads its own -fg, so there is no exception left to go stale, and the five fills are in contrast:check as non-text pairs — 184 now, up from 174.

And a drawn boundary, because two fills are not one

Contrast is a luminance ratio, and these five separate by hue at near-equal lightness. Measured against each other, the closest pair is 1.01:1 in light and 1.06:1 in dark. That is deliberate — a series that is much darker than its neighbours dominates a stack it should not — but it means a greyscale print, or a reader with no colour vision, sees one block where the legend names three states.

So the segments carry a 1px separator in the card colour, and the boundary is drawn rather than inferred from the two fills either side.

It is not enough to check the four neighbouring pairs. A month with no In-Progress work makes New touch Quoted; a month with only two states makes any pair adjacent. All ten pairs are possible, so the answer has to be structural, not a palette tweak.

Cancelled also keeps a chart-only token, --vy-chart-cancelled. As badges, New and Cancelled sit on separate rows and a near-identical grey costs nothing; in a stacked bar they touch — and measured, the badge greys are 1.35:1 apart in light and identical in dark.

My Queues — a count you can act on

Four measures, each a link into the Quotations list already filtered.

TileMeans
OverduePast the due date and still open
Due this weekDue within the next seven days
UnassignedOpen, with nobody carrying it
Waiting on a documentA checklist task with no document attached yet

One predicate, imported twice

The four tests live in data/queues.ts. Both this page and the Quotations list import them.

A tile that reads 8 overdue, clicked, opening a list of six is the bug this prevents — with nothing on screen to say which number is wrong.

Measured on the seeded set: the Overdue tile reads 8, and the list it opens reads 1 - 8 of 8 items. They cannot disagree, because there is only one rule.

The tile's own sentence travels with the link. The list arrives carrying From My Queues · Overdue · Team · Past the due date and still open, and a Clear. A filtered list that does not say why it is filtered is a list that looks short.

Which is also why the sentence has to keep up with the test. Waiting on a document read "a checklist task is started but its document has not arrived" until 9 November — a description of the predicate it used to have and no longer does. The test had been corrected months earlier, because the old one leaned on a status the guideline had stopped deriving; its sentence stayed behind, and then travelled to the list banner as well. One predicate in one place is only half the rule — the words beside it have to move with it.

Mine and Team is a scope, not a role

Two states, both always available, so nobody has to work out which one they are entitled to see. First visit defaults to Mine (decision D4) — the narrower scope is the one that is always safe to show — and the choice persists per browser.

Team adds one thing Mine cannot have: who is carrying the most, as bars. The question a manager opens this page to ask is comparative, and reading five numbers and ranking them is work the page should do.

The four states, and why zeros are not one of them

StateWhat the screen shows
LoadingFour skeleton tiles, in the real tile shape
ErrorYour queues could not be loaded · what it means · Try again
EmptyOne sentence — Nothing outstanding — and a link onward
ReadyThe counts

Two of these exist because of the same mistake:

Rendering 0 while the counts load says "nothing needs you" at the exact moment it does not yet know. It is not a neutral placeholder; it is a confident wrong answer, and it arrives first.

And when the answer really is nothing, that is a sentence too — not four zeros. Four zeros make the reader check each one to work out that the total is nothing. The sentence says it once, and names the scope: Switch to Team to see everyone else's.

The header bell follows the same rule from the other end. It counts what is late and mine, not everything outstanding.

A badge showing total workload is permanently lit, and a permanently lit badge stops being read.

What is deliberately not here

Per-module queues. data/moduleQueues.ts groups queues by the module they belong to, so Overdue under Procurement is a late purchase order and Overdue under Sales is a late RFQ, with the heading saying which. It is written, and nothing imports it.

Customer decision, 25 Aug 2026: "My Queue is low priority — for now just note that we will have it in the future." It is kept rather than deleted because it is most of the way done; what it still needs is the role from a JWT rather than a local preference, and real record types behind the four modules whose counts are currently illustrative. The file says so at the top, which is the difference between parked and abandoned.

Accessibility

The chartrole="img" with a label naming the year and the total, so the shape is not the only way to get the figure
Every barCarries its count as text under it and in the legend — the picture is a second reading, never the only one
Series colourNever alone: each has a legend entry naming the status, and the segments are separated by a drawn line, not by hue
ZeroDimmed to --vy-on-surface-subtle, which is a text tier that still clears 4.5:1 — not hidden, and not faint
TilesReal links, so middle-click and open-in-new-tab work; the count, the label and its meaning are all text
Scope togglerole="group" with a name, aria-pressed on each button
Loadingaria-busy on the row, and each skeleton tile names the measure it is standing in for
Errorrole="alert", with the retry as a real button
