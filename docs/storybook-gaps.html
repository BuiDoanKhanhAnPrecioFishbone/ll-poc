The BA's principles of 16 Nov 2026 name roughly 60 widgets and states across her five widget sections. The gap below is measured against those.

A · Components that exist but have no live example — 10

These are built, used in the app, and documented in prose. They are missing only the thing you can click.

ComponentWhy it is not there yet
data-gridThe heaviest one: the Grid chunk is 663 KB and a specimen has to seed its own rows. Also the one most worth having
column-chooser · filter-toolbar · view-picker · view-settingAll four are grid satellites. Each needs the grid around it to make sense, so they follow the grid
people-pickerNeeds a people fixture; otherwise straightforward
command-paletteOpens over the whole page, so a specimen needs a frame it can own
app-shellA specimen of the shell is a specimen of the whole app. Probably a screenshot rather than an iframe
iconShould be a grid of all 45, which is easy and overdue
support-ticketStraightforward

Three of these also have no tab structure at all — icon, app-shell and command-palette have a page but not the four-tab layout every other component has.

Suggested order: icon first (easy, high use), then support-ticket and people-picker, then the grid and its four satellites as one piece, then command-palette. app-shell last, and probably not as an iframe.

C · Widgets the BA specifies that do not exist here at all

This is not a storybook gap — there is nothing to put in it. These are components to build, and the first two groups need Kendo packages that are not installed.

~~Dates~~ and ~~numbers~~ — built, 17 Nov 2026

Date, Datetime, Date range, Integer, Decimal, Currency and Percent — seven of her widgets, none of which existed. See Number field and Date field.

Only one package was needed in the end, @progress/kendo-react-dateinputs, and it was already in node_modules as a transitive dependency; it is now declared directly. Numbers did not take @progress/kendo-react-inputs — the reason is on the Number field page.

A correction to this page. It listed Switch among the missing widgets. Her spec names Switch only to rule it out — the row reads "Not used" — so there is nothing to build, and building one would have added a control she has decided against. The row is a decision, not a gap.

~~Yes / no field~~ and ~~Multi-column list~~ — built, 17 Nov 2026

The last two of her Input widgets. The yes/no field is a boolean that reads as a word written for its field — Required / Not required — which is what separates it from a bare checkbox. The multi-column list is a lookup whose popup is a small grid, and it exists for one reason: 0455-300-0017-11 and 0455-300-0017-12 differ in the last character of sixteen.

It also put --vy-grid-header in the system, one more of her values. In light it is what Kendo was already using; in dark hers is lighter than ours was.

~~Cards and groups~~ — built, 17 Nov 2026

All four: Card and Split card, Group, Section heading and Collapsible group. The division she draws is the useful part — a card is a surface of its own, a group divides one surface, and her whole note on the group is "no box, no fill".

It gave --vy-section-bg its first use. The token had nowhere to go while this system had no card.

Tab was already here but did not match her spec, which only measuring showed: 33px tall, touching, and 400 weight for both states, so the active tab was marked by its underline alone. Now 36px, 24px apart, 500 with the active at 600. She writes 22px apart and 22 is not on the spacing scale — not on ours and not on hers, which agree step for step — so it takes the 24px step, written down rather than quietly rounded.

~~Images and conversation~~ — built, 17 Nov 2026

File row with its five type chips and four states, Image box in both states, and Conversation, which also covers Notes — she specifies Notes as "As Conversation", so building two would only guarantee they drift.

Still open from that section: the image popup and the file Preview are both her Popup Large 960px, which is a dialog size this system already has; the dialog that goes in it is a screen, not a component. Email is her Popup Medium 640px with inputs that now all exist.

Two things to raise with her. She gives no dark values for the file-type chips — these use her dark values for the nearest family she names. And her chip text is 9px, below the 12px desktop floor her own type scale sets; it is kept because a three-letter mark in a fixed 30×20 box is recognised rather than read, and it is named --vy-text-chip so the exception is visible instead of buried in a rule.

What this adds up to

A and B are finishing work on things that already exist — a few days, no decisions, and they make the storybook genuinely complete for what the system holds today.

C is product scope. Seven of those widgets are now built; the rest need components designed from scratch. That is a plan to agree with her and with you, not a gap to quietly fill — and it is worth saying to her that her own spec is ahead of the build in these four areas, since she may be specifying for a milestone this mockup was never meant to reach.
